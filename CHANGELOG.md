# Changelog

All notable changes to Local Pre-Rolls Pro. Versions follow `major.minor.patch`; Jellyfin shows them with a fourth `.0`.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

First release.

- Pre-rolls from your own folders (with subfolders), scanned automatically
- Rules by genre, tag, studio, person, rating, library, collection, series, viewer, content type, resolution, HDR / Dolby Vision, audio format, date (yearly or one-off), release date and year, weekday, time of day and day of month
- Pre-show builder: rule picks, fixed bumpers and local trailers of unwatched movies
- Per-title pre-rolls from a `.prerolls` folder, a no-pre-roll list and a `no-preroll` tag
- Age-safe selection, new pre-roll boost, no-repeat, cooldowns and binge detection
- Per-file settings on the settings page or in sidecar JSON files
- Automatic H.264/AAC copies of files apps can't play, loudness measurement and optional volume matching
- Built-in prerolls.me catalog with thumbnails, previews, one-click downloads, auto-tagging and subscriptions
- Test tab that explains exactly what plays and why
- Help tab, full input validation, backup/restore and import from Local Intros
