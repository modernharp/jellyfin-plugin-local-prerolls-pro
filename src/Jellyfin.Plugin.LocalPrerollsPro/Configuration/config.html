How often

Compatibility and volume

For HEVC, AC3, HDR, 10-bit or AVI files the plugin makes an H.264/AAC MP4 copy in the background and plays that. It prevents most "stuck after the pre-roll" problems.

Viewers who never get pre-rolls

Free pre-rolls from prerolls.me, made by the community for personal media servers. Please keep the creator credit. Videos hosted by prerolls.me download straight into your pre-roll folder with their tags. Ones hosted on YouTube or Vimeo can only be opened on the website.

Subscriptions

Follow a category or tag and new hosted pre-rolls download automatically. The plugin checks about once an hour and downloads at most 20 per check. Pre-rolls you delete are not downloaded again.

Getting started

  1. Put your pre-roll videos in a folder the Jellyfin server can read. MP4 files with H.264 video and AAC audio work best.
  2. On the Files tab, enter that folder and click Save and scan. Your pre-rolls are listed below it. You can also download some from the Catalog tab.
  3. A rule called "All pre-rolls" already plays a random pre-roll before every movie and episode. Add more rules on the Rules tab when you want different pre-rolls for different titles, dates or viewers.
  4. In your Jellyfin app, turn on Cinema mode (Settings → Playback). Play a movie from the start.
  5. If nothing plays, open the Test tab, pick the movie, and it tells you why.

How a pre-roll is chosen

  1. Should this title get a pre-show at all? Not if it's on the no-pre-roll list or has the skip tag, its type is turned off, the viewer is excluded, the viewer had a pre-show recently, or the episode rule says no.
  2. Which pre-rolls are allowed today? Pre-rolls that are turned off, outside their dates, or rated above the title (with age-safe on) are left out.
  3. The pre-show runs, step by step. In a "Rule picks" step, matching rules with the highest priority win, and weight decides the odds between their pre-rolls. If the title has its own .prerolls folder, those pre-rolls are used instead.

Questions

Nothing plays before my movie
  • Turn on Cinema mode in the app (Settings → Playback). It is a per-device setting.
  • Pre-rolls never play when you resume a title, or for the second item onward in a play queue.
  • Some apps only ask for pre-rolls before movies, not episodes.
  • Open the Test tab and test the title. It shows exactly what stopped the pre-show.
The pre-roll plays, but the movie doesn't start, or there's a long pause

This usually happens when the app has to transcode the pre-roll. Keep Make compatible copies on (Playback tab) and look for warnings on the Files tab. For the best results use MP4 files with H.264 video and AAC stereo audio.

My folder shows "Not found on the server"

Enter the folder exactly as the Jellyfin server sees it. With Docker that is the path inside the container (for example /media/prerolls), not the path on your computer. On Windows, use a full path such as D:\Prerolls. The Jellyfin user needs permission to read the folder.

What do priority and weight mean?

Priority: when several rules match, only those with the highest priority are used. Example: "Christmas" at priority 10 beats the "All pre-rolls" rule at priority 0 during December.

Weight: among the rules that are used, a pre-roll with weight 3 is picked three times as often as one with weight 1. You can also give single pre-rolls a weight with Edit on the Files tab.

How do I set up holiday pre-rolls?

Put them in a subfolder such as christmas/, or tag them "christmas" with Edit. On the Rules tab choose Add a holiday rule → Christmas, tick "Everything in christmas/" or the tag, and click Keep rule, then Save. Its priority (10) beats the catch-all rule only during those dates.

Can a movie or show have its own pre-roll?

Yes. Make a folder named .prerolls inside the movie's folder, the series folder, or a season folder, and put the videos in it. They play only for that title. Choose on the Playback tab whether they replace the rule picks or play first.

How do I keep a title free of pre-rolls?

Add it under Titles that never get pre-rolls on the Playback tab, or give it the tag no-preroll in Jellyfin's metadata editor. A series or season on the list covers all its episodes.

How does age-safe work?

Give a pre-roll a rating with Edit on the Files tab, for example R for a horror pre-roll. With age-safe on, it never plays before a title rated lower, such as PG or a kids' show. Pre-rolls without a rating play before anything. For titles without a rating, you choose on the Playback tab.

Can I set pre-roll details in files instead of on this page?

Yes. Next to xmas.mp4 create xmas.mp4.json (or xmas.json):

{ "name": "Christmas Bumper", "tags": ["christmas"], "rating": "G",
  "weight": 2, "from": "12-01", "to": "12-26", "enabled": true }

A prerolls.json in a folder sets defaults for every file in it:

{ "defaults": { "tags": ["christmas"] },
  "files": { "santa.mp4": { "rating": "G" } } }

Dates are MM-dd to repeat every year, or yyyy-MM-dd for one year only. Settings made with Edit override the file. If a file has a mistake, the scan result on the Files tab names it.

What do the loudness numbers mean?

Loudness is measured in LUFS. Closer to 0 is louder. Movie dialogue is usually around -24. A pre-roll at -10 is much louder than your movie. Turn on Volume-match on the Playback tab and the plugin plays an adjusted copy instead. Your original file is never changed.

Where are converted and volume-matched copies kept?

In Jellyfin's cache folder, under local-prerolls-pro. They are made in the background after each scan and deleted automatically when the original is removed or the settings change. Originals are never modified.

What does "Repair library items" do?

Jellyfin can only stream files it knows about, so the plugin keeps a hidden library item for each pre-roll. Repair removes and recreates those items. Your files and settings are not touched. You only need it if pre-rolls stop playing after a database problem.

How does the plugin treat prerolls.me?
  • The catalog is downloaded at most once an hour and kept on your server. Searching and filtering never contact the website.
  • Thumbnails are fetched once, at most four at a time, and kept on your server.
  • Downloads happen one at a time. A subscription check downloads at most 20 pre-rolls and runs about once an hour.
  • If the site can't be reached, the plugin waits 5 minutes before trying again.
  • Each downloaded pre-roll keeps the creator credit and a link to its page. The clips are meant for personal media servers.
I used the Local Intros plugin before

Open the Backup tab and click Import Local Intros settings. Then disable or uninstall Local Intros (Dashboard → Plugins), otherwise both plugins add pre-rolls.

Episodes in a binge keep getting pre-rolls

On the Playback tab, set TV episodes to "Only the first episode of a binge" or "Once per series per day". You can also set a number of minutes between pre-shows.
